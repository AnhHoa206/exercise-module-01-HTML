A Story About Myself

\

Since I was a child, I have had a strong interest in Japanese language and culture. Watching anime and reading manga made me curious about Japan, and I dreamed of visiting the country someday. Because of this interest, I decided to study Japanese at the University of Foreign Language Studies in Da Nang. During my university years, I actively participated in school activities and tried to improve my communication and teamwork skills. One of the most memorable experiences in my life was my internship at a hotel in Japan. I worked in different areas, including housekeeping, restaurant service, and front desk support. At first, I was nervous because everything was new and I had to communicate in Japanese. However, whenever I did not understand something, I tried to research it by myself and ask experienced colleagues for help.
